### ⚠ BREAKING CHANGES

* **sqlitehost/csharp:** ISqliteHostConnection.Query<T> is renamed to QueryRows and erased to object. Adapter implementations rename the method, replace T with object, and change List<T> to List<object>; callers keep using the Query<T> extension unchanged. See docs/adapter-contract.md, migration note.
